\section{Research design}
The proposed outcome is the continuous score from the nine-item Patient Health
Questionnaire (PHQ-9), which asks about symptoms in the previous two weeks.
We would measure it in successive cross-sections of Peru's Encuesta Demográfica
y de Salud Familiar (ENDES).

\paragraph{Candidate estimand.}
For eligible residents of localities that gain usable service, we propose to
estimate the average effect in PHQ-9 points at a given time since local
activation, relative to deferring activation until after the interview.
Localities with verified later activation could provide comparisons while they
are still awaiting service. The coauthors will agree on comparison eligibility,
event-time windows and weights after reviewing the available sample. If the
records support only regional timing, a separate proposal would estimate the
effect of the regional launch offer among designated localities.

\paragraph{Exposure timing.}
We will record roster designation, regional operations, institutional acceptance
and observed service delivery as distinct milestones. Public Wi-Fi, household
connectivity and individual use measure different forms of access. We will compare
the documented dates or date bounds for usable institutional service with the
actual interview timing and symptom recall period. Observations with ambiguous
ordering will be flagged before any exposure coding is finalized.

\paragraph{Sample support.}
The archived pilot of 17 November 2026 is the benchmark for rebuilding the sample.
The Stata reconciliation will compare unweighted counts of eligible respondents,
eligible rural respondents, rural roster matches and distinct matched localities
by survey round. The original input vintages are still being recovered.
